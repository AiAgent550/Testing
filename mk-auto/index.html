How a remap works

Four steps, and your original file is always kept.

STEP 1

Health check

Scan for faults and check the car is fit to tune before we touch anything.

STEP 2

Read and back up

The original software is read and stored, so the car can always go back to standard.

STEP 3

Write the map

The tuned file goes on, matched to your car and any modifications.

STEP 4

Road test

Live data logging on the road to confirm everything runs as it should.
